A little help,
whenever you need it.

Nutritious helps you keep a private food journal, notice your mood and energy, and collect meal ideas and delivery links.

Your journal

Your entries stay on your device. In Preferences, use Export journal to save a JSON copy through your device’s share or download options. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes local records.

Starting with a photo

On iPhone or iPad, choose Log a meal → Add a photo. The system picker shares only your selected image, and a bundled model checks it locally. Clear photos of some foods can produce a suggestion; mixed dishes often need manual entry. Confirm any suggested label and choose food groups yourself. If the app cannot identify the food, enter the meal manually. Photos are not saved in your journal. A photo cannot confirm hidden ingredients, allergens, portions, or nutrients. In the browser edition, photos provide a temporary reference for manual entry only.

Delivery links

Use your delivery app’s Share → Copy link option, then add it in Nutritious. We recognize HTTPS links from Meituan, Ele.me, Uber Eats, and DoorDash. Some shortened links are not supported. A saved link opens with its original provider; Nutritious does not manage your delivery account, prices, cart, or order.

Food information

Food groups describe the ingredients you select. Meal ideas are general inspiration, not verified nearby restaurant listings or a nutrient assessment. Restaurant recipes and portions vary. Confirm actual ingredients and cross-contact with the restaurant if you have an allergy.

Get support

Open a support request ↗

Support requests on GitHub are public and require a GitHub account. Describe the issue and your device/iOS version. Do not include private diary entries, health information, passwords, or payment details.
