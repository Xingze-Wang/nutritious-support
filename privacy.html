Privacy, in plain language.

Effective October 3, 2026. Nutritious is provided by Xingze Wang.

What stays on your device

Meals, food-group selections, portions, diary notes, mood and energy check-ins, preferences, ingredient exclusions, water entries, and saved delivery links are stored locally. The iOS app uses its local app preferences; the browser edition uses browser storage. Nutritious has no journal server or account system and does not send these records to the developer.

No tracking or advertising

The app includes no advertising SDK, analytics service, or third-party AI service. Bundled photos and fonts load locally. There is no cross-device account sync.

Sharing and delivery providers

You choose when to export your journal or share a meal brief. iOS presents its system share sheet so you can choose a destination. A journal export includes your journal records and preferences. A meal brief includes its recipe ingredients and your selected ingredient exclusions, but not diary notes or mood entries. Temporary export files are removed from the app’s cache after sharing finishes.

Clicking a delivery link opens the provider’s website or supported installed app. Nutritious does not receive the provider’s account credentials, payment details, cart, or order history. Your interactions with the provider are governed by its own policies. Saved provider links may contain identifiers originally included by that provider.

Retention, deletion, and backups

You control local retention. Delete entries in your journal or use Preferences → Clear local data to remove all Nutritious records and settings on that device. Browser storage can also be removed by clearing browser data. Uninstalling the iOS app removes its local data. Your device’s OS backup settings may include app data; Nutritious does not independently upload backups. Exported copies remain wherever you choose to save or share them, and must be deleted there separately.

Security

Native records are kept inside the app’s local storage. Nutritious does not separately encrypt exported JSON files or browser storage. Use your device lock and share exports only with recipients you choose.

This website and support requests

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its privacy statement. Public support requests are handled through GitHub Issues. Information you post there is visible publicly and may be used to diagnose and respond to the issue. Do not post sensitive personal or diary information.

Children and health information

Nutritious is a general food journaling tool, not a service specifically directed to children. It does not diagnose or treat health conditions, infer deficiencies, or claim that food changes caused a mood or energy change.

Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated. For support or privacy questions, use the Nutritious support page. Do not include sensitive data in a public issue.
